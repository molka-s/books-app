About Us

At Booktopia, we’re passionate about transforming how readers discover, organize, and enjoy books. Founded on the idea of blending technology with the love of reading, Booktopia is your intelligent bookstore and book management assistant. Our AI-driven platform learns your preferences to bring personalized book recommendations, streamline your searches, and simplify your library management. Whether you’re an avid reader, a casual browser, or a collector, Booktopia is here to enhance every step of your reading journey. Join us in exploring a world where books meet innovation, making every reader’s experience more enriching and enjoyable.

OUR BLOG

Modern Mrs. Darcy (Anne Bogel)

Known for her blog on books and lifestyle, Anne Bogel offers thoughtful reviews, reading lists, and reading challenges. Her "What Should I Read Next?" podcast is also a favorite among book lovers looking for personalized book recommendations.

The Literary Edit (Lucy Pearson)

Based in the UK, Lucy Pearson's blog covers a wide range of literary topics, including book reviews, literary travel, and author interviews. Her blog is especially known for beautifully curated recommendations and a focus on literary fiction and memoirs.

The Perpetual Page-Turner (Jamie C.)

Jamie’s blog combines book reviews with personal reflections on life, making it relatable and engaging. She covers various genres, from YA and fantasy to contemporary fiction, and shares reading lists and bookish tips for every type of reader.
